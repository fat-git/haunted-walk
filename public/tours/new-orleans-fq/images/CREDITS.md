# Image credits — French Quarter Ghosts tour

This directory holds the stop images used by `public/tours/new-orleans-fq/tour.json`.
Every image is hosted on Wikimedia Commons under a free license (CC0, public domain,
CC-BY 2.0/3.0/4.0, or CC-BY-SA 2.0/3.0/4.0), was taken from a public place, and shows
the stop it is paired with. Credits and licenses are copied from each file's Commons
page; sourceUrl links to that page.

Stops without an image have none because no suitable free-licensed image could be
verified for them: St. Louis Cemetery No. 1 (view-from-outside rule limits usable
photos to the Basin Street wall; nothing verifiable was found) and The Rising Sun
Hotel Site (no image of 535 Conti Street could be verified).
